### Downtime is Over 
We decide to make our way to the fire cult

As we travel, Gusty trips 
Investigating the plank, we find a whole world?! (metaphor)
I see an old tin cup and a battered coffee pot. Sitting off the road is a pick, spade, and saddle. A small book lies half buried in the desert sand, buzzards fly over and the wind blows harshly over it. There's the rusty rim from a wagon near the board that Gusty tripped over 
The book is an old tattered journal, signed by J.T. Earnest, a miner chasing gold. We find a guitar the man played. The man had many dreams and plans to get away from it all, build a new life, etc. We find a handful of nuggets gleaming in the sun, and his gun. He learned from a friend that after a long travel, following a rainbow in the sky, who found a pot of gold but it was fool's gold. A small piece of paper falls out. A faded pic of a girl, wearing a gingham dress, next to a letter about how a stranger had come and taken her away and how her love for the miner has dissipated. The journal ends with him praying next to his battered stuff and his many plans 
On the front of the book are 2 engraved lines, one long one short, resembling a T (a cross). It's a Christian, it's a King James bible 
We make our camp. There's some shrubbery, some agave, a small spring, sounds of a burrowing owl hooting not far off.
